Call for Papers

The 1st International Workshop on Embodied AI Quality and Safety (EQualS 2027) invites contributions on the quality and safety of embodied AI systems, including vision-language-action (VLA) models, robot foundation models, learning-enabled robotic systems, and autonomous agents. As failures can propagate across software, learned models, and physical execution, task success alone is insufficient to establish system quality and safety. With software engineering as the primary lens, the workshop brings together software engineering, robotics, and AI safety communities to explore systematic approaches to testing, verification, monitoring, and assurance.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

Testing and Evaluation

  • Embodied AI, VLA, and robot foundation model testing
  • Scenario generation and search-based testing
  • Test oracles, metamorphic testing, coverage, and adequacy

Safety and Dependability

  • Safety specification, verification, and validation
  • Reliability, robustness, and runtime assurance
  • Runtime monitoring, fault injection, diagnosis, and recovery

Interaction and Environments

  • Security and adversarial testing
  • Simulation, digital twins, and sim-to-real validity
  • Human-robot interaction safety

Evidence and Engineering Practice

  • Quality and safety benchmarks and metrics
  • Reproducible failure reporting
  • Processes and tools for trustworthy embodied AI

Submission Guidelines

We solicit extended abstracts only, with a maximum of five pages including references. We welcome early-stage results, emerging ideas, negative results, practical experience, benchmarks, tools, and research challenges suitable for workshop discussion.

Submission Categories

Category Pages Suitable contributions
Research extended abstracts 3–5 Research contributions and early-stage results on embodied AI quality and safety.
Position or vision extended abstracts 2–5 Perspectives, emerging ideas, and open research challenges.
Benchmark or experience extended abstracts 2–5 Benchmarks, practical experience, and lessons from evaluating or assuring embodied AI systems.
Demo extended abstracts 2–3 Demonstrations of tools, systems, benchmarks, or preliminary results.

All page limits include references.

Formatting instructions

All submissions must be written in English, submitted in PDF format, and prepared using the ACM two-column conference proceedings format. Authors must use the ACM Proceedings Template. LaTeX users should use the following document class: 

\documentclass[sigconf,screen,review,anonymous]{acmart}

Authors must adhere to the specified page limits and must not modify the template's font sizes, margins, or spacing to circumvent these limits.

Review process

All submissions will undergo a double-anonymous review process by members of the Program Committee. Submissions will be evaluated based on relevance, originality, clarity, soundness appropriate to the contribution's maturity, and potential to stimulate discussion.

Submissions must not reveal the authors' identities. Authors should remove names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and other identifying information, refer to their own previous work in the third person, and anonymize any supplementary materials or artifacts provided for review.

Proceedings and Publication Policies

Accepted extended abstracts will be included in the FSE 2027 workshop proceedings and published in the ACM Digital Library, subject to the FSE publication process. All accepted contributions will be explicitly classified as extended abstracts and are free of Article Processing Charges (APCs), in accordance with the FSE 2027 workshop instructions.

Registration and Participation

At least one author of each contribution appearing in the proceedings must register for the workshop. Attendance is open to all registered workshop participants, subject to room capacity. An accepted submission is not required to attend the workshop.

The workshop program will include paper presentations, poster and demo sessions, an invited talk, a panel discussion, and facilitated working sessions. For further information, see the Workshop Schedule and Registration and Venue.

Submission Eligibility and ACM Policies

EQualS organizers and their directly supervised students, postdoctoral researchers, and employees may not submit to EQualS, in accordance with ACM SIGSOFT policy.

As a published ACM author, you and your co-authors are subject to all ACM Publications Policies, including ACM's new Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects.
