Call for Papers

EQualS 2027 invites contributions on the quality and safety of embodied AI systems, including vision-language-action (VLA) models, robot foundation models, learning-enabled robotic systems, and autonomous agents.

Failures in these systems can propagate across software, learned models, and physical execution. Task success alone does not establish robustness, reliability, or safety. The workshop connects software engineering, robotics, and AI safety perspectives on how to test, verify, monitor, and assure these systems.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

Testing and Evaluation

  • Embodied AI, VLA, and robot foundation model testing
  • Scenario generation and search-based testing
  • Test oracles, metamorphic testing, coverage, and adequacy

Safety and Dependability

  • Safety specification, verification, and validation
  • Reliability, robustness, and runtime assurance
  • Runtime monitoring, fault injection, diagnosis, and recovery

Interaction and Environments

  • Security and adversarial testing
  • Simulation, digital twins, and sim-to-real validity
  • Human-robot interaction safety

Evidence and Engineering Practice

  • Quality and safety benchmarks and metrics
  • Reproducible failure reporting
  • Processes and tools for trustworthy embodied AI

Submission Guidelines

We solicit extended abstracts only, all at most five pages including references. We welcome early-stage results, emerging ideas, negative results, practical experience, benchmarks, tools, and research challenges suitable for workshop discussion.

Review and Selection

Each submission will receive at least two independent reviews from non-conflicted Program Committee members, with three reviews where capacity permits.

Selection will consider relevance, originality, clarity, soundness appropriate to the contribution’s maturity, and potential to stimulate discussion. The program is planned for approximately 10–14 accepted extended abstracts, including 2–3 demonstrations. These figures are planning estimates; contributions will be selected through peer review.

Accepted authors will prepare a brief claim-and-evidence summary identifying the quality or safety challenge addressed, the evidence or evaluation method used, and one unresolved question. These summaries will support cross-community working groups at the workshop.

Proceedings and Publication Policies

Accepted extended abstracts will be included in the FSE 2027 workshop proceedings and published in the ACM Digital Library, subject to the FSE publication process.

All contributions will be explicitly classified as extended abstracts. In accordance with the FSE 2027 workshop instructions, these contributions are free of Article Processing Charges (APCs).

Registration and Eligibility

At least one author of each contribution appearing in the proceedings must register for the workshop. The APC exemption concerns publication of extended abstracts; it does not waive this registration requirement.

Attendance is open to all registered workshop participants, subject to room capacity. An accepted submission is not required to attend or to participate in discussions and working sessions. Registration arrangements are described under Registration and Venue.

EQualS organizers and their directly supervised students, postdoctoral researchers, and employees may not submit to EQualS, in accordance with ACM SIGSOFT policy.

As a published ACM author, you and your co-authors are subject to all ACM Publications Policies, including ACM's new Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects.
