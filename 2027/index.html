The 1st International Workshop on Embodied AI Quality and Safety (EQualS 2027)

Welcome

Welcome to The 1st International Workshop on Embodied AI Quality and Safety (EQualS 2027), co-located with FSE 2027.

This workshop addresses two complementary dimensions of embodied AI quality and safety:

  1. Testing and Evaluation of Embodied AI Systems: systematic testing of vision-language-action (VLA) models, robot foundation models, learning-enabled robotic systems, and autonomous agents through scenario generation, test oracles, coverage criteria, simulation, and quality benchmarks.
  2. Safety Verification and Assurance for Embodied AI Systems: safety specification, verification, runtime monitoring, fault diagnosis and recovery, and human-robot interaction safety, with attention to the evidence supporting system-level safety claims.

Grounded in a software engineering perspective, EQualS brings together researchers and practitioners from software engineering, embodied AI, robotics, AI safety, and human-robot interaction to advance systematic methods for testing, evaluation, monitoring, and assurance.
